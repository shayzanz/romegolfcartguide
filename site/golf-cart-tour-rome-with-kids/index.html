Golf cart tours in Rome with kids

Kids love the cart, parents love not carrying anyone. Here's what to know before you book.

Updated October 2026

Quick answer

Golf cart tours are great for families in Rome: kids enjoy the ride and nobody gets tired of walking. Some operators provide free child seats for ages 2–12 but don't accept infants under 2, while others allow babies in a pram or stroller. Private tours with hotel pickup and a gelato stop are the easiest option.

Ages and child seats

  • Policies differ by operator. One popular operator provides child seats or boosters for free for ages 2–12 and doesn't accept infants under 2 for safety reasons.
  • Other tours allow infants in a pram or stroller, and some are marked as not suitable for babies. Check the listing.
  • Always tell the operator each child's age when booking so the right seats are ready.

Making it fun for kids

  • Pick a tour with gelato. It's the highlight for most children.
  • Ask about the stories. Gladiators at the Colosseum, chariot races at Circus Maximus, and throwing a coin into the Trevi Fountain.
  • Try the Aventine keyhole. Peeking through it to see St. Peter's dome is a favourite.
  • Keep it short for young kids: a 1.5–2.5-hour tour is plenty.

Practical tips

  • Private tours let you stop for bathroom breaks and snacks whenever you need.
  • Bring hats and water in summer.
  • Larger families can book two carts on some tours, up to 14 people.

Questions people ask

Are golf cart tours in Rome suitable for children?

Yes. They're one of the most family-friendly ways to see Rome. Check each tour's age policy and request child seats.

Can I bring a baby?

It depends on the operator: some don't accept infants under 2, others allow babies in a pram or stroller.

Which tour is best for kids?

A private tour with hotel pickup and a gelato stop, ideally 2.5–3 hours or less.

Quick answer

Golf cart tours in Rome start at about $69 per person for a 1.5-hour shared tour. Classic 3-hour shared tours cost about $100–150 per person, private tours about $81–230 per person ($400–800 per cart), and cruise day trips from Civitavecchia start around €790 per group.

What's usually included

  • The electric cart and a driver-guide, usually English-speaking
  • Often bottled water and headsets so you can hear the guide
  • On some tours: gelato, coffee, a pasta tasting, or hotel pickup

Usually not included: entry tickets to monuments (tours stop outside), meals unless stated, and tips.

What makes it cheaper or pricier

  • Shared vs private: the biggest difference. Shared is cheaper for 1–3 people.
  • Group size: private tours get cheaper per person as you add people, up to about 6–7.
  • Length: express tours cost about half of a 3-hour tour.
  • Extras: food, hotel pickup and catacomb entry add to the price.

How to save

  • Two people? Book a shared express or classic tour.
  • Five to seven people? Compare the private per-cart price; it's often the cheapest per head.
  • Pick tours with free cancellation so you can change plans if the weather turns.

Questions people ask

How much is a golf cart tour in Rome per person?

About $69 per person for a 1.5-hour shared tour, about $100–150 for a classic 3-hour shared tour, and about $81–230 per person on private tours, depending on group size.

How much is a private golf cart tour in Rome?

Roughly $400–800 for the whole cart for 2.5–3 hours, usually with hotel pickup. Per person it gets cheaper the more people share the cart.

What's the cheapest golf cart tour in Rome?

Shared express tours of about 1.5 hours start from around $69 per person.

Is a golf cart tour in Rome worth the money?

For most first-time visitors, yes: you see 6–8 major sights in about 3 hours without walking 10+ km, which is especially useful in summer heat or with kids and older travellers.

Do I need to pay entry tickets?

Not for the standard tour, which stops outside the monuments. Tickets are extra if you want to go inside the Colosseum or museums.

Should I tip?

It's optional. €5–10 per person, or about 10% on a private tour, is common.

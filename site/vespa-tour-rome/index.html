Vespa tours in Rome

Rome from the back of a Vespa, or from a sidecar. Here's how the different formats compare and which ones travellers rate highest.

Updated October 2026

Quick answer

Most Vespa tours in Rome are 1.5–3 hours with a professional driver: you ride as a passenger or in a sidecar, so no licence is needed. Highly rated options include the 1.5-hour Highlights Vespa Sidecar Tour with Gelato (4.9/5 from 844 reviews on GetYourGuide) and the 3-hour Sidecar Tour with Hotel Transfers & Gelato (5/5 from 268 reviews).

Ratings and review counts as shown on GetYourGuide in November 2026.

Three ways to do a Vespa tour

  • Sidecar: you sit in a sidecar next to the driver. The easiest to get in and out of, and the most photogenic.
  • Passenger with a driver: you ride behind a professional driver, who is also your guide. Private and flexible.
  • Self-ride: you drive your own Vespa behind a guide. You need a valid licence and real scooter experience: Rome traffic is not the place to learn.

Vespa or golf cart?

A Vespa is more fun and more "Roman Holiday", but it's best for one or two people who are comfortable on a scooter. A golf cart is better for families, older travellers, anyone with a bag or a camera to hold, and rainy days, since most carts have a roof.

Tips

  • Helmets are provided and mandatory. Wear closed shoes and avoid long, loose clothes.
  • Night tours are cooler in summer and the lit-up monuments look great from a moving Vespa.
  • Tell the operator your height and weight if you're unsure about fitting in a sidecar.

Questions people ask

Do I need a licence for a Vespa tour in Rome?

Not if you ride as a passenger or in a sidecar, which is how most tours work. Self-ride tours require a valid licence.

How long is a Vespa tour in Rome?

Usually 1.5 to 3 hours.

What is the best Vespa tour in Rome?

Among the highest rated on GetYourGuide are the Highlights Vespa Sidecar Tour with Gelato (4.9/5, 844 reviews) and the Sidecar Tour with Hotel Transfers & Gelato (5/5, 268 reviews).

Vespa or golf cart: which is better?

Vespa for couples who want an adventure; golf cart for families, seniors and anyone who prefers a seat with a roof.

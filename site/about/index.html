About this guide

Run from Rome, to help visitors find the right golf cart tour quickly.

Why this guide exists

There are dozens of golf cart tours in Rome, sold across several booking sites, and they look almost identical at first glance. The differences that matter, like hotel pickup, group size, length, food stops and price, are buried in long listings. We put them side by side so you can choose in a minute.

How we compare tours

  • Public information only. We use what operators and booking platforms publish: price, duration, inclusions, pickup area, group size and cancellation policy.
  • Popularity and track record. We favour operators with a long history of bookings and consistently strong ratings on the major platforms.
  • Fit, not just price. We group tours by who they suit: families, seniors, cruise passengers, food lovers, people on a budget.
  • Regular updates. Prices and details change, so we review listings regularly and show the update date on each page.

How we make money

When you book through our links, the booking platform pays us a small commission. It costs you nothing extra and doesn't change the price you pay. For tours we arrange directly on WhatsApp, the local operator pays us a commission. We never accept payment to rank a tour higher.

What we are not

We don't run tours or own golf carts. Each tour is run by a licensed local operator, and bookings made through Viator or GetYourGuide are handled by those platforms, including payment, changes and refunds.

Rome's Christmas lights by golf cart

December evenings in Rome are magical, and cold. A night tour by cart is the easiest way to see the lights.

Updated October 2026

Quick answer

Rome's Christmas lights usually go on around 8 December and stay up until Epiphany on 6 January. A 2–3 hour evening golf cart tour can cover the lights on Via del Corso and Via dei Condotti, the trees at the Spanish Steps and Piazza del Popolo, the Christmas market in Piazza Navona, and the tree and nativity scene in St. Peter's Square.

What to see

  • Via del Corso: lights stretch along the main shopping street between Piazza Venezia and Piazza del Popolo.
  • Via dei Condotti and the Spanish Steps: the luxury street leading to the Steps, usually with a big tree at the top.
  • Piazza del Popolo: in recent years home to the city's main Christmas tree.
  • Piazza Navona: Rome's traditional Christmas market, with stalls, sweets and a carousel, open until Epiphany.
  • St. Peter's Square: the Vatican's giant tree and life-size nativity scene, usually lit in early December.
  • Trastevere: lights strung between the ivy-covered buildings, perfect for dinner afterwards.

Key dates

  • Around 8 December (Feast of the Immaculate Conception): the city's lights and main trees are usually switched on.
  • Early December: the St. Peter's Square tree and nativity are usually unveiled.
  • Until 6 January (Epiphany): lights, markets and decorations stay up.

Exact dates change every year, so check the city's official announcements closer to the time.

Why by golf cart

  • The lights are spread across the center: on foot it's several kilometres in the cold.
  • Carts reach the narrow streets where the best lights are.
  • Most carts have a roof and some operators provide blankets. Ask when booking.

Tips for a December evening tour

  • Book a slot starting around 5–6pm, just after dark.
  • Dress warmly: open-sided carts get cold, and December evenings in Rome are often around 5–10°C.
  • Avoid 24–25 December and 31 December unless the operator confirms it's running.
  • For a private Christmas lights route, message us and we'll arrange it with a local operator.

Questions people ask

When are the Christmas lights on in Rome?

Usually from around 8 December until Epiphany on 6 January.

Where are the best Christmas lights in Rome?

Via del Corso, Via dei Condotti and the Spanish Steps, Piazza del Popolo, Piazza Navona's Christmas market and St. Peter's Square.

Is there a Christmas market in Rome?

Yes, the traditional market in Piazza Navona, usually open until 6 January.

Is a golf cart tour good in December?

Yes, if you dress warmly. Evening tours are the best way to see the lights without walking several kilometres in the cold.

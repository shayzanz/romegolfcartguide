Rome's Christmas lights 2026: dates, best spots and how to see them

The 2026 dates we could confirm, where the lights are, and a simple evening route that covers them without walking for kilometres in the cold.

Updated 20 November 2026

Quick answer

In 2026 the Christmas tree in St. Peter's Square is lit on Monday 7 December at 5:30pm. The Piazza Navona Christmas market can open from 1 December and runs until Epiphany, 6 January 2027. The lights on Via del Corso were installed in early November and are usually switched on in early December. The best spots are Via del Corso, Via dei Condotti and the Spanish Steps, Piazza Venezia, Piazza Navona, St. Peter's Square and Trastevere. A 2–3 hour evening golf cart tour covers most of them.

This year's Vatican tree is a 24-metre white fir from the Pollino National Park in southern Italy. It will be lit at the same moment as the giant tree on Monte Ingino in Gubbio.

Where to see the lights

  • Via del Corso: the main shopping street, lit from Piazza Venezia towards Piazza del Popolo. It's pedestrian-heavy in the evening, so see it from the side streets or on foot for a few minutes.
  • Via dei Condotti and the Spanish Steps: the luxury street and the square below the Steps, usually the most photographed decorations in the city.
  • Piazza Venezia: where the city's official tree usually stands, in front of the Vittoriano.
  • Piazza Navona: the traditional Befana market, with stalls, sweets and a carousel, open late into the evening.
  • St. Peter's Square: the Vatican's tree and nativity scene, lit every evening until the end of the Christmas season.
  • Trastevere: lights strung across the narrow streets. A good place to end the evening with dinner.

A simple evening route

  1. 5:00pm, Piazza Venezia: start just after dark at the city tree.
  2. Via del Corso and Via dei Condotti: the lit shopping streets, then the Spanish Steps.
  3. Trevi Fountain: lit all year and quieter after dark than at midday.
  4. Piazza Navona: stop at the Christmas market.
  5. St. Peter's Square: across the river for the tree and the nativity.
  6. Trastevere: finish with dinner.

On foot this is around 6–7 km. By golf cart it fits into 2–3 hours, including photo stops.

Why a golf cart works well in December

  • The lights are spread across the whole center. A cart links them without the long walk in the cold.
  • Carts can reach narrow streets where buses and cars can't go.
  • Most carts have a roof, and some operators provide blankets. Ask when you book.

Tips

  • Book a start time around 5–6pm, just after sunset (about 4:40pm in December).
  • Dress warmly. December evenings in Rome are often around 5–10°C and carts are open at the sides.
  • 7 and 8 December and the weekends before Christmas are the busiest evenings. Book a few days ahead.
  • On 24, 25 and 31 December, check that the operator is running before you plan around it.

Questions people ask

When are the Christmas lights switched on in Rome in 2026?

The St. Peter's Square tree is lit on 7 December 2026 at 5:30pm. The Via del Corso lights were installed in early November; the city usually switches the lights on in early December and keeps them up until Epiphany on 6 January.

When does the Piazza Navona Christmas market open in 2026?

It can open from 1 December 2026 and closes on 6 January 2027. The exact opening day is usually announced in late November (it opened on 6 December in 2025 and 8 December in 2024).

Where are the best Christmas lights in Rome?

Via del Corso, Via dei Condotti and the Spanish Steps, Piazza Venezia, Piazza Navona, St. Peter's Square and Trastevere.

Where does the 2026 Vatican Christmas tree come from?

It's a 24-metre white fir from the Pollino National Park, a gift from the town of Terranova di Pollino.

Is a golf cart tour a good idea in December?

Yes, if you dress warmly. An evening tour links the lights in 2–3 hours, instead of 6–7 km on foot in the cold.

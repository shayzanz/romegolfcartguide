The best golf cart tours in Rome for 2026

Quick answer

The best golf cart tour in Rome overall is a private city highlights tour with hotel pickup (rated 4.9/5 on GetYourGuide). For value, the 1.5-hour Biga Tours express tour from $69 is hard to beat; for families, the VIP tour with Trevi skip-the-line, gelato and supplì (4.8/5 from 858 reviews); and for evenings, the Rome By Night private tour (4.9/5).

Ratings and review counts are as shown on GetYourGuide in October 2026. We check them regularly.

1. Best overall: Private Guided City Highlights Tour

A private cart, an English-speaking guide and pickup included, rated 4.9 out of 5 from 250 reviews. It covers the classic sights (Colosseum, Pantheon, Trevi, Piazza Navona) at your pace. The safest choice for a first visit.

2. Best for families: VIP Golf Cart with Trevi Skip-the-Line, Gelato & Supplì

The most reviewed tour in our list, 4.8 out of 5 from 858 reviews. Kids love the gelato and the supplì (Rome's fried rice ball), and skip-the-line access at the Trevi Fountain saves time in the busiest spot of the day. Private, 2–2.5 hours.

3. Best small-group tour: Trevi Access, Gelato & Supplì

The shared version of the tour above, 4.9 out of 5 from 297 reviews. Same treats and Trevi access at a lower price, with other travellers on board.

4. Best for big groups and a custom route: Rolling Rome private tour

Rolling Rome is one of the most established golf cart operators in the city, with thousands of reviews across platforms. You choose the stops, they pick you up at your central hotel, and they can run two carts for groups of up to 14. A full-day version with lunch is available.

5. Best value: Biga Tours Rome Express

From $69 per person for 1.5 hours, it's the cheapest way to see the headline sights by cart. Small groups (max 7 per cart), headsets and water included, free child seats for ages 2–12.

6. Best night tour: Rome By Night Golf Cart Tour

Two hours through Rome lit up after dark, private with pickup included, 4.9 out of 5 from 161 reviews. Guides speak English, Italian, Spanish or German. See our night tour guide.

7. Best with drinks: Evening Golf Cart Tour with Aperitivo

A 3-hour sunset-to-evening tour that ends with an Italian aperitivo, 4.8 out of 5 from 389 reviews. Small group or private.

8. Best beyond the center: Appian Way, Catacombs and Aqueducts

A 4-hour small-group tour (max 5 people) out along the ancient Appian Way, with a catacomb visit and the Roman aqueducts, 4.8 out of 5. See our catacombs guide.

Questions people ask

Is a golf buggy tour the same as a golf cart tour?

Yes. "Golf buggy" is the British term for the same small electric vehicle. In Rome the tours are identical: an open cart for 4–7 people with a driver-guide.

What is the best golf cart tour in Rome?

For most visitors, a private city highlights tour with hotel pickup. The Private Guided City Highlights Tour by Golf Cart is rated 4.9/5 from 250 reviews on GetYourGuide.

What's the best golf cart tour in Rome for families?

The VIP golf cart tour with Trevi skip-the-line, gelato and supplì: private, 2–2.5 hours, rated 4.8/5 from 858 reviews.

What's the cheapest good golf cart tour in Rome?

The Biga Tours Rome Express, about 1.5 hours from $69 per person.

Is there a good night golf cart tour in Rome?

Yes. The Rome By Night private tour is 2 hours with pickup included and rated 4.9/5 from 161 reviews.

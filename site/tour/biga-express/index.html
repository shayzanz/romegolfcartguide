Rome Express Golf Cart Tour

1.5 h · Shared small group · Meeting point in the center

About this tour

The quickest way to see the headline sights: Pantheon, Trevi, Piazza Navona and the Colosseum from outside. Starts near the Pantheon. Free child seats for ages 2–12.

The tour starts from the operator's office near the Pantheon and ends outside the Colosseum. Carts seat 7 and a tour runs with up to two carts.

Max 7 per cartHeadsetsWater

Indicative route

Stops and order change with traffic, closures and, on private tours, your wishes. This is the typical route for this kind of tour, not a fixed itinerary.

  1. Start near the PantheonMeeting point in the center (Via Monterone).
  2. PantheonUsually a short walk in: carts can't reach the door.
  3. Piazza NavonaBernini's Four Rivers fountain.
  4. Trevi FountainTime to throw your coin.
  5. Piazza VeneziaThe white Vittoriano monument.
  6. ColosseumPhoto stop and the story of the gladiator games.

Good to know

  • Tours stop outside the monuments; entry tickets are not included unless listed.
  • You meet the guide at a central meeting point, shown in your booking confirmation.
  • Tell the operator about children's ages or a wheelchair when you book.
  • Tips are not included and are up to you.

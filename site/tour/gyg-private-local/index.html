Indicative route

Stops and order change with traffic, closures and, on private tours, your wishes. This is the typical route for this kind of tour, not a fixed itinerary.

  1. ColosseumPhoto stop and the story of the gladiator games.
  2. Circus MaximusThe ancient chariot track below the Palatine Hill.
  3. Aventine Hill keyholeThe famous keyhole view of St. Peter's dome and the Orange Garden.
  4. Mouth of TruthThe marble mask from the film Roman Holiday.
  5. Theatre of MarcellusOlder than the Colosseum, in the Jewish Ghetto.
  6. Piazza VeneziaThe white Vittoriano monument.
  7. PantheonUsually a short walk in: carts can't reach the door.
  8. Piazza NavonaBernini's Four Rivers fountain.
  9. Trevi FountainTime to throw your coin.
  10. Spanish StepsMany tours end here or at your hotel.

Good to know

  • Tours stop outside the monuments; entry tickets are not included unless listed.
  • Add your hotel name and address when booking, ideally 24 hours ahead.
  • Tell the operator about children's ages or a wheelchair when you book.
  • Tips are not included and are up to you.

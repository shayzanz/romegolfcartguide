Rome by golf cart from Civitavecchia cruise port

One day in port, the whole of Rome, and no stress about getting back to the ship.

Updated October 2026

Quick answer

Golf cart shore excursions from Civitavecchia include a private transfer to Rome (about 1–1.5 hours each way), around 5 hours touring the center by golf cart, and the return to your ship on time. They're private for your group and start from about €790 per group.

How the day works

  • Pickup at the port. Your driver meets you at Civitavecchia, Rome's cruise port, timed to your ship.
  • Transfer to Rome by car or van, about 80 km.
  • About 5 hours by golf cart through the historic center: Colosseum, Pantheon, Trevi, Piazza Navona, the Jewish Ghetto and back streets big buses can't reach.
  • Back to the ship with a safe margin before all-aboard.

Why it suits cruise passengers

  • You see far more than on a coach tour, because carts get into the narrow streets.
  • No train timetables or taxis to sort out.
  • Private: your group only, at your pace, with lunch stops on request.

What to tell us when you ask

  • Ship name and date in port
  • Arrival and all-aboard times
  • Number of people, and anyone with limited mobility

Questions people ask

How far is Rome from Civitavecchia?

About 80 km. The transfer takes about 1–1.5 hours each way depending on traffic.

How much is a golf cart shore excursion from Civitavecchia?

From about €790 per group for a private day with transfers and around 5 hours by cart.

Will we be back to the ship on time?

Cruise-day operators plan the schedule around your all-aboard time and leave a safety margin.

Golf cart tours for seniors and limited mobility

Rome is hilly, cobbled and spread out. A golf cart takes the walking out of it.

Updated October 2026

Quick answer

Golf cart tours are one of the easiest ways for seniors and people with limited mobility to see Rome: the cart drives right up to the main sights and most private tours include hotel pickup. Many operators accept wheelchairs; a folded wheelchair usually takes the space of two seats, so mention it when booking.

Why it works

A classic walking tour of Rome easily means 10 km or more on cobblestones, with steps and hills. On a golf cart you ride between the sights and only get off for photos, sometimes without leaving your seat. The Pantheon is one of the few places where a short walk is usually needed, because carts can't reach the door.

Wheelchairs and walkers

  • Several tours are listed as wheelchair accessible. The passenger transfers to a seat and the folded chair travels on the cart.
  • A folded wheelchair takes about two seats, so a 5-seat cart has room for 3 passengers plus the chair.
  • Electric wheelchairs usually don't fit. Ask the operator first.

How to choose

  • Go private so you set the pace and get out only when you want.
  • Pick hotel pickup so there's no walk to a meeting point.
  • Choose morning or evening in summer to avoid the heat.
  • Tell the operator about any needs when booking: they'll plan the route and the stops.

Questions people ask

Are golf cart tours in Rome good for elderly travellers?

Yes, they're one of the most comfortable ways to see the city: no long walks, shade from the cart's roof, and hotel pickup on most private tours.

Can I bring a wheelchair on a golf cart tour?

Usually a folded manual wheelchair, which takes about two seats. Tell the operator when you book.

Do you have to walk at all?

Very little. Most sights are seen from the cart or a few steps away; the Pantheon usually needs a short walk.

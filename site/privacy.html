Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data romegolfcartguide.com ("we", "us") handles, why, and what rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Italian data protection law.

Who is responsible for your data

This website is run by RomeGolfCartGuide.com. For any question about your data, or to exercise your rights, contact us on WhatsApp at +39 393 855 2086.

What we collect

Browsing this site. We don't use advertising cookies or tracking pixels, and we don't ask you to create an account. Like every website, our hosting provider automatically records technical data (IP address, browser type, pages requested, date and time) in server logs to keep the site secure and working.

WhatsApp messages. If you contact us on WhatsApp, we receive your phone number, your name as shown in WhatsApp and whatever you write to us, for example travel dates, group size and hotel area. We use this only to answer you and help you find or arrange a tour.

Bookings with a local operator. If you ask us to arrange a tour that isn't sold online, we share with that tour operator only what they need to run it: your name, phone number, date, group size and pickup address. We tell you who the operator is before you confirm.

Visit statistics (only with your consent). If you click "Accept" in the cookie banner, we use Google Analytics 4, a service of Google Ireland Limited, to count visits and understand how the site is used: which pages are read, which country and type of device visitors use, and which buttons are clicked (for example "See dates & book" or WhatsApp). Google Analytics sets the cookies _ga and _ga_*, which last up to 2 years. IP addresses are not stored by Google Analytics 4, we have turned off advertising features, and we don't use this data to identify you. If you click "Reject" or ignore the banner, Google Analytics is not loaded at all.

Links to booking platforms

When you click "See dates & book", you leave our site and go to Viator or GetYourGuide. The link contains our partner ID so the platform can pay us a commission. These platforms may set cookies and will process your booking and payment data under their own privacy policies. We never see your payment details, and they only tell us that a booking was made, not who you are.

Third-party content on this site

To show photos and fonts, your browser loads files from Unsplash (images) and Google Fonts. These providers receive your IP address when the files load. They act under their own privacy policies.

Legal basis

  • Answering your messages and arranging tours you ask for: steps taken at your request before or to perform a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Visit statistics with Google Analytics: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and art. 122 of the Italian Privacy Code). You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Server logs and site security: our legitimate interest in running a safe website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Keeping records we're required to keep by law, such as invoices: legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

How long we keep it

WhatsApp conversations are kept for up to 12 months after your last message, unless they relate to a booking, in which case we keep the booking details as long as tax and accounting law requires (generally 10 years in Italy). Server logs are kept for a short period set by our hosting provider. Google Analytics data is kept for 14 months, then deleted automatically.

Where your data goes

WhatsApp, our hosting provider, Unsplash, Google Fonts and Google Analytics may process data outside the European Economic Area. In those cases they rely on safeguards approved by the European Commission, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, to limit or object to how we use it, or to receive it in a portable format. Just write to us using the contacts above; we'll reply within one month. If you think we've handled your data unlawfully, you can complain to the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

Cookies

We use two kinds of storage:

  • Technical (no consent needed): your browser remembers your cookie choice (key rgcg_consent in local storage) for 6 months, so we don't ask you again on every page.
  • Analytics (only with consent): the Google Analytics cookies _ga and _ga_* described above.

We don't use advertising or profiling cookies. You can change your choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page; if you reject, we delete the Google Analytics cookies. Viator and GetYourGuide set their own cookies only after you leave our site and go to theirs.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

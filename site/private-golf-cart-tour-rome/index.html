Private golf cart tours in Rome

Your own cart, your own guide and your own route. Here's what a private tour costs and which ones are worth booking.

Updated October 2026

Quick answer

A private golf cart tour in Rome usually lasts 2.5–3 hours and costs about $81–230 per person, or roughly $400–800 for the whole cart. Most include pickup at hotels in the historic center, and you can choose the stops.

What makes a tour "private"

On a private tour the cart, the driver and the guide are only for your group. In practice that changes three things:

  • You choose the route. Want more time at the Colosseum and skip the Spanish Steps? Just say so.
  • You choose the pace. Stop for photos, an espresso or a gelato whenever you like.
  • They usually pick you up. Most private tours collect you at your hotel if it's in the historic center, often inside the ancient Aurelian Walls.

Private or small group?

If you're two people on a budget, a shared small-group tour (from $69) is great value. Go private if you're a family or group of 4 or more, travelling with someone who can't walk far, celebrating something, or simply want to decide the stops yourself.

Tips before you book

  • Add your hotel name and address when booking. Many operators need it at least 24 hours ahead to plan the pickup.
  • If your hotel is outside the center, ask for the nearest pickup point.
  • Larger groups can book two carts: some operators take up to 14 people.
  • Tips aren't included. About 10% is common on a private tour if you enjoyed it.

Questions people ask

How much is a private golf cart tour in Rome?

From about $81 per person for an entry-level 3-hour tour, up to around $230 per person for premium options. Priced per cart, expect roughly $400–800.

Do private golf cart tours include hotel pickup?

Most do, for hotels in Rome's historic center. If your hotel is further out, the tour starts from a central meeting point.

How many people fit in a private golf cart?

Carts usually seat 5–7 passengers. Some operators send a second cart so groups of up to 14 can tour together.

Can we choose the route on a private tour?

Yes. That's the main advantage: tell the guide which sights matter to you, or let them suggest a route.

Vintage Fiat 500 tours in Rome

The little 1960s car that became a symbol of Italy, with a local behind the wheel. Here's how to pick the right tour.

Updated October 2026

Quick answer

Vintage Fiat 500 tours in Rome usually last 1.5–3 hours with a local driver, and cars take 2–3 guests. The most reviewed is the City Tour in a Retro Fiat 500 with Photo Stops (4.9/5 from 952 reviews on GetYourGuide). If you want to drive yourself, there are convoy tours, which require a valid driving licence.

Ratings and review counts as shown on GetYourGuide in November 2026.

With a driver or behind the wheel?

  • With a driver (most tours): a local drives and tells stories, you enjoy the ride and the photos.
  • Self-drive convoy: you drive a vintage Fiat 500 behind a guide's car. Fun, but the cars have manual gearboxes and Rome traffic is intense. A valid driving licence is required; non-EU visitors should check whether an International Driving Permit is needed.

Fiat 500 or golf cart?

A Fiat 500 is the more iconic, photogenic choice for a couple. A golf cart fits more people (usually 5–7), is easier to get in and out of, and often includes hotel pickup.

Tips

  • The cars are small: travel light and tell the operator if anyone is very tall.
  • Photo tours are popular for proposals and anniversaries; book a few days ahead.
  • Older cars have little or no air conditioning, so in summer pick a morning or evening slot.

Questions people ask

How long is a Fiat 500 tour in Rome?

Usually 1.5 to 3 hours.

Can I drive the Fiat 500 myself?

Yes, on convoy tours, with a valid driving licence. Most tours have a local driver instead.

How many people fit in a vintage Fiat 500?

Usually 2–3 guests plus the driver.
